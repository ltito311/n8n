Reservation & Cancellation Policy

Reservation Confirmation

All reservations are confirmed via email or phone within 24 hours of booking. We recommend arriving 10-15 minutes before your reservation time.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations must be made at least 4 hours in advance. Same-day cancellations may be subject to a cancellation fee for groups of 6 or more.

No-Show Policy

Tables are held for 15 minutes past the reservation time. No-shows may affect future reservation availability.

Large Groups

Parties of 8 or more require advance notice and may have limited menu options. A service charge may apply for groups of 10 or more.
