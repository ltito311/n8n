Jerk Chicken Wings

Appetizer

$12.99

Experience the authentic taste of Jamaica with our signature jerk chicken wings. Marinated for 24 hours in our secret blend of scotch bonnet peppers, allspice, thyme, and aromatic spices, these wings are grilled to perfection and served with our house-made cooling mango dip.

Each order features 8 premium chicken wings, perfectly seasoned and grilled over open flames to achieve that distinctive jerk flavor and smoky char that makes this dish a true Caribbean classic.

Spice Level: Medium-Hot
Gluten-Free Available
Prep Time: 15 minutes

Chef's Notes

"The secret to authentic jerk chicken lies in the marinade time and the balance of heat and sweet. We marinate our wings for a full 24 hours to ensure the flavors penetrate deep into the meat. The mango dip isn't just a cooling agent - it's carefully crafted to complement the complex spice profile while highlighting the tropical essence of Jamaica."

- Chef Marcus Thompson, Executive Chef
